# Landing page (src/app/page.tsx)

Scope: public landing page, Persuade mode. Visitors: Indian adults, mostly on phones, deciding whether to trust a stranger with their last words. Action: start free (3 people) or understand the price. Proof on hand: the mechanism itself; no testimonials exist.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is an India Post inland letter card, opened. It refuses the category's two defaults: the black "digital vault" with shields and locks, and the cream-paper sympathy card.

OWN-WORLD: Inland-letter blue paper as the whole ground, indigo printing ink for all type, India Post red for printed rules, perforations and the stamp frame, violet rubber-stamp ink only for postmarks. Voices: Anek (the printed card), Kalam (the person's hand), Martel (letters being read). Bilingual printed labels, fold and cut lines.

STORY: Visitor sees a letter addressed to the people they love, learns it only moves if they stop checking in, sets a rhythm and watches when reminders and release would happen, sees what a recipient receives, sees ₹0 for 3 and ₹20 for 20 printed as stamps, starts writing.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The card's address face fills the screen: printed masthead top left, stamp and violet postmark top right (the postmark carries the check-in date), handwritten "To, the people I love" address block at large scale centre left, one printed sentence explaining the mechanism, and the primary action "Start writing, free" set in the sender's-address box at the bottom. A dashed "fold here" line crosses below.
